# The 184 defects of DTUGAME.EXE 1.3 in the port

The list of defects is D:\dev\DTU-Decomp `docs/defects.md` (2026-11-22). This table gives the state of
each one in the port on 2026-11-23. The answers to three open points of that list (D-057, D-074,
D-101) are in DTU-Decomp `docs/resolved-2026-11-23.md`.

Columns:

- Mark: how the defect was verified in the decompilation. "bytes": read in the raw bytes of
  `local/dtu13.elf`. "bytes (2026-11-23)": read in the bytes for this table. "pseudocode": read in
  the pseudocode only. "in part": one part in the bytes, another part not. "subsystem": by the
  subsystem document of DTU-Decomp, not read again. "second pass": by the second pass of the name
  table. "not verified": the claim of the source note. "disputed": the code contradicts the note.
- State: "corrected": the port has a `Dtu_fix_*` correction (the setting, without the prefix, and the
  option that clears it; each setting was checked in the code: defined with the value 1).
  "copied": the port reproduces the 1.3 defect. "not applicable": DOS only, editor, dead code, a part
  that the port replaced, or not a defect of 1.3 (the note says which). "port differs": the port
  does something else with no named correction. "unknown": the behavior was not found in the port.
- Note: the decision for a copied row ("Kept" with the reason, or "Open"), then the place in the port.

The states come from a read of the port code for each defect (2026-11-23). Only the rows with the
mark "bytes" are acted on; the others need a verification in the bytes first.

## Open rows

- Copied and met by a player: D-035 (pseudocode), D-040 (pseudocode; the intended roll cannot be
  read from the code), D-090, D-097, D-099, D-106, D-112, D-114, D-134, D-138, D-139, D-141, D-146,
  D-158, D-165, D-169, D-171 (all by the subsystem document or not verified). Each needs a
  verification in the bytes before a correction.
- Port differs: most of these rows are a crash, a fault or a leak of 1.3 that the port does not
  have (D-010, D-033, D-038, D-060, D-066, D-136, D-150, D-178, D-180 to D-182), or a part that the
  port does in another way (the renderer, the input, the sound). The ones that a player sees and
  that need a decision: D-012 (the hit sound of the killing blow), D-050 and D-054 (model textures),
  D-053 (the splash light), D-089 and D-095 (character creation), D-098 (Slow), D-131 (trap timers
  in a pause), D-140, D-147, D-154, D-157, D-160 (interface), D-167 (3D sound volume). None of them
  has the mark "bytes".
- D-002 and D-003 (port differs, mark "bytes"): the port clamps the THAC0 row at level 18 and gives
  creatures the plain strength bonus. Both are rare (level 18; a creature with exceptional strength)
  and the port does the correct thing; a named correction with the 1.3 rule is still to add.
